# Product sample feedback revision list: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The brand, the supplier, the product, the people and every date and note are made up. A product lead at an invented consumer brand is reconciling the feedback on the second sample of an insulated lunch tote from Supplier A.

## What the person supplies

**Concept brief** (product: insulated lunch tote; features and requirements):

- Capacity: fits two 500 ml containers and a sandwich box.
- Closure: opens with one hand.
- Strap: comfortable to carry, adjustable.
- Lining: wipe-clean.
- Colour: sage green.

**Sample register** (Sample ID, Version, Date received, Supplier, Labelled, Supplier-stated changes):

- S-01, V1, 2026-09-14, Supplier A, yes, first sample.
- S-02, V2, 2026-10-12, Supplier A, yes, zip replaced with a magnetic flap; strap lengthened by 6 cm; inner pocket added; lining unchanged.

**Earlier revision requests** (Request ID, Feature, Requested change, Sent, Supplier's reply):

- R-1, Closure, replace the zip, which snags on the lining, 2026-09-22, "done in V2".
- R-2, Strap, lengthen the strap, 2026-09-22, "done in V2".
- R-3, Lining, stitch the base seam flatter, 2026-09-22, "will review".
- R-4, Colour, move the green toward the sage swatch, 2026-09-22, "done in V2".

**Feedback notes** (Note ID, Date, Sample ID, Source, Feature, Comment):

- N-01, 2026-09-18, S-01, Tester 1, Closure, "Zip snags on the lining every second try."
- N-02, 2026-09-18, S-01, Tester 2, Strap, "Too short to carry over a coat."
- N-03, 2026-09-19, S-01, Tester 1, Lining, "Seam at the base catches crumbs."
- N-04, 2026-10-16, S-02, Tester 1, Closure, "Magnetic flap opens with one hand. Good."
- N-05, 2026-10-16, S-02, Tester 2, Closure, "Flap opens too easily. A container slid out when the bag tipped."
- N-06, 2026-10-17, S-02, Customer panel 1, Strap, "Long enough now, but the buckle digs into my shoulder."
- N-07, 2026-10-17, S-02, Customer panel 2, Strap, "Buckle is sharp on the shoulder. Add padding."
- N-08, 2026-10-17, S-02, Tester 2, Colour, "Too grey. I would prefer a brighter green."
- N-09, 2026-10-18, S-02, Customer panel 1, Colour, "Love the muted green."
- N-10, 2026-10-18, S-02, Tester 1, Lining, "Seam at the base still catches crumbs."
- N-11, 2026-10-19, S-02, Customer panel 2, Lining, "Smells strongly of chemicals when new. Is it safe for food?"
- N-12, 2026-10-19, (blank), Tester 2, Capacity, "The green bag from the second box fits three containers."
- N-13, 2026-10-20, S-02, Customer panel 1, Capacity, "Fits two 500 ml containers and the sandwich box with room to spare."

**Settings:** agreed means two different people; the product owner resolves conflicts and approves the brief; the materials and safety questions go to the person the brand names for them (no name supplied); date of the run, 2026-10-22.

Every line traces to the notes, register, brief and requests supplied.
